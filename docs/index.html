Reading the attribution

  • Greeks: opening sensitivities applied to the change in spot, implied volatility, rates and time.
  • Approximation residual: full BSM repricing less the Greek estimate. Includes higher-order effects.
  • Market/model basis: mark-to-market P&L less BSM repricing. Check valuation inputs and timestamps.
  • Coverage: fixed contract quantities and European BSM pricing. Exercise, settlements and trades are excluded.

METHODOLOGY

Model assumptions

European option prices with continuous dividends. Scenario attribution uses opening Greeks and full model repricing.

BLACK–SCHOLES–MERTON

Closed-form pricing with continuous dividends

S is the underlying spot, K the exercise price, T the time in years, r the risk-free rate, q the dividend yield and σ the annualized volatility.

C = S e−qT N(d₁) − K e−rT N(d₂)
P = K e−rT N(−d₂) − S e−qT N(−d₁)

d₁ = [ln(S/K) + (r − q + σ²/2)T] / (σ√T); d₂ = d₁ − σ√T.

01 / EXERCISE

European contracts

Options are assumed exercisable only at expiration. This model does not include American early exercise.
